# Function Goal Taxonomy

## 1. RETRIEVAL
**Definition:** Accessing existing data.

* **SEARCH:** To discover.
* **ACQUISITION:** Obtain/fetch data from a known identifier.

---

## 2. ANALYSIS
**Definition:** Deriving structural, relational, or subset insights without altering source data.

* **FILTERING:** Holding back elements.
* **SORTING:** Ordering elements based on some characteristic.
  * **RANK:** Ordering elements by relative standing, priority, or score within a group.
  * **ALPHANUMERIC:** Ordering based on mixed alphabetic and numeric characters within a string.
  * **NUMERIC:** Ordering based on sequential numbering.
  * **ALPHABETIC:** Ordering based on alphabetical sequence.
* **EXAMINATION:** Inspection or observation.
  * **VERIFICATION:** Confirmation of some defined criteria.
  * **COMPARISON:** Evaluation of similarity and difference between two things.

---

## 3. TRANSFORMATION
**Definition:** The alteration of data.

* **CALCULATION:** Computation of numbers.
* **CONVERSION:** Modifying the form of something.
  * **REPRESENTATION:** Alteration of machine data types and structures.
  * **VALUE:** Value possession of the variable.
* **UPDATES:** Active modification to data within the state of a system.
  * **ADDITION:** Extending or adding new information to the state of the system on an already existing artifact.
  * **REMOVAL:** Remove information from the state of the system.
    * **DELETION:** Singular removal.
    * **CLEARING:** Complete removal.
  * **MODIFICATION:** Replacing existing attribute values in place.

---

## 4. GENERATION
**Definition:** The act or instance of producing new content that did not exist in another format beforehand (i.e., generating new images, files, videos, etc.).

---

## 5. EXECUTION
**Definition:** Putting something into effect, like executing a command.

---

## 6. INACTION
**Definition:** Non-functional or no goal.

---

## 7. SIMULATION
**Definition:** Imitative representation of the functioning of one system or process.
